AI Assistant

Let the AI reply to your profiles' conversations and lead new ones. Replies look like normal profile messages — no badge, sent after a natural delay.

Status

When on, incoming messages to your profiles get an automatic reply after a short delay. Toggle it off any time to take back over manually — you can still see and open every conversation in the Conversations tab.

AI provider

Choose the AI provider that writes replies using your knowledge base as reference. With no provider configured, saved knowledge-base replies are used instead.

Select Groq and provide its API key to have the AI compose every reply from your knowledge base. The AI searches all topics as reference and rewrites the answer in its own words — saved replies are never sent verbatim. Retired Groq models are skipped automatically, so leaving the model blank is safest.
The AI waits a random time in this range as a "thinking" pause, then adds realistic typing time based on how long the reply is (shorter replies arrive sooner, longer ones take longer). A typing bubble shows while it composes.

Lead conversations

The AI reaches out to listed members who have no conversation yet, one at a time, using the "opener" replies from your knowledge base.

Works while this admin dashboard is open. The AI picks a different opener each time so no two leads get the same greeting.

Knowledge base

The AI reads these topics and replies as reference material, then writes a response that fits the conversation instead of copying a saved reply. Without a selected provider, matching saved replies are used. The opener topic is used when leading new conversations.

Add a new topic

Admins

Admins sign in with their email on index.html and are routed to this dashboard. Add more admins below.

Import profiles (JSON)

Upload a JSON file of profiles to bulk-create or update them in one go. After importing you can still add and edit profiles normally in the Profiles tab.

Format: a JSON array of objects. Each object needs fullName and username; everything else is optional. Matching username (or an id) updates an existing profile instead of adding a new one.

Delete chat history

Permanently remove chats from Supabase. Choose the age of the chats to delete — everything older than the chosen age is wiped.

The "Delete all chats" option wipes the entire message history for every user and profile.

Data tools

Clears all users, profiles, messages and notifications, then re-seeds fresh demo data. Cannot be undone.

Points & earnings

Control how members earn points, the daily chat goal, referral rewards, conversion rates, and the account activation fee.

Changing the currency relabels amounts; existing balances and points are not converted.
This note appears in the Rewards & Earnings section before a member activates. Set the fee to 0 for free activation.
Defaults: 100 points = Ksh 30.00 ≈ USD 0.23.

Payment methods

Methods marked Connected are usable in the app. Card always shows "Coming soon". Binance deposits are verified manually here — go to Payments to confirm them.

M-Pesa charges the activation fee as whole shillings (KES). The activation fee and currency are configured under Points & earnings.
